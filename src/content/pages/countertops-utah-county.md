## Countertops in Utah County, Fabricated Ten Minutes Away

Graebridge Stone's shop sits at 15 S 1400 W in Lindon, which puts most of Utah County within a short drive for templating, installation, and the occasional "can you come look at this?" visit. ### Why a local fabricator matters

Every slab we sell is cut, edged, and polished in Lindon by our own team, not shipped to a regional plant. That means you can see your actual slab before it is cut, we can lay out seams and veining with you in the room, and if something needs adjusting on installation day the shop is minutes away. Most kitchens are installed in a single day.
