## Kitchen Countertops, Templated and Installed in One Day

The kitchen is where a countertop earns its keep: hot pans, homework, holiday dinners, and the daily coffee ring. Graebridge Stone has fabricated kitchen surfaces for more than thirty years, and we build each one to fit your cabinets, your sink, your cooktop, and the way your family actually uses the room.

We work in [quartz](/quartz-countertops/), [granite](/granite-countertops/), [quartzite, marble, and soapstone](/quartzite-marble-countertops/), and large-format [porcelain](/#porcelain), fabricated in our Lindon shop and installed across Utah County, the Salt Lake Valley, and the Park City and Heber area.

### What we build

- **Full kitchen countertops** with sink and cooktop cutouts, polished edges, and seams placed where they disappear.
- **Islands and waterfall edges** with mitered corners and book-matched veining that flows from the top down the side.
- **Full-height backsplashes** in the same slab as the counter, for a seamless, easy-to-clean wall.
- **Countertop replacement and retrofit** over existing cabinets, with your old tops removed and hauled away.
- **Tile and backsplash coordination** so the whole wall reads as one design.

### Choosing a material for a working kitchen

Quartz is the most popular choice for busy households because it never needs sealing and hides nothing in the pattern. Granite and quartzite are the natural-stone options that stand up to heat and daily use. Porcelain is the newest option: thin, light, UV-stable, and nearly impossible to scratch, and we are one of the few Utah shops certified to fabricate and install it. Marble and soapstone bring unmatched character to kitchens whose owners are happy to let the surface age.

### Edges, finishes, and the details that matter

Edge profiles include eased, bevel, bullnose, ogee, and mitered waterfall. Finishes run from polished to honed to leathered. Undermount sinks, integrated drainboards, and flush cooktops are all standard work for us. We talk through all of it before anything is cut.

### Our process

1. **Consult.** Bring photos and rough measurements to the Lindon shop, or send them by text. Free estimate.
2. **Template.** We measure every edge, cutout, and overhang precisely.
3. **Fabricate.** Cut, edged, and polished by our own team on the slab you chose.
4. **Install.** Most kitchens are done in one day. We remove the old countertops, set the new ones, and connect nothing you did not ask us to touch.

### Frequently asked questions

**How long does a kitchen countertop installation take?** Usually one day on site. Templating happens about one to two weeks before, depending on material availability.

**Do I have to remove my old countertops?** No. Removal and haul-away are included.

**Can you match my existing backsplash or cabinets?** Yes. Bring a cabinet door or a tile sample to the shop and we will lay slabs beside it.
