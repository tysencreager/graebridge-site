## Countertops for Park City, Heber City, and the Heber Valley

Mountain homes ask more of a countertop: bigger islands, bolder stone, and a finish that holds up to a full house every ski weekend. Graebridge Stone has fabricated for Park City and Heber Valley homes since the beginning, and the drive over the hill from our Lindon shop is one we make often. We are family-owned and woman-led, licensed and insured, and certified in large-format porcelain, with more than thirty years of stone experience behind every project.

### Communities we serve on the Wasatch Back

Park City · Heber City · Midway · Kamas · Daniel · Hideout · Coalville · Silver Summit · Summit Park · Snyderville Basin · Jordanelle, and the surrounding mountain communities.

### What mountain homeowners ask for

- **Quartzite and granite islands** with dramatic veining, book-matched across waterfall edges, for great rooms built around the kitchen. See [Quartzite & Marble](/quartzite-marble-countertops/) and [Granite](/granite-countertops/).
- **Large-format porcelain** for fireplace surrounds, shower walls, and outdoor kitchens that have to survive freeze-thaw winters and high-altitude sun. See [Porcelain](/#porcelain).
- **Marble baths and wet bars** in primary suites and bunk-room baths. See [Bathroom Countertops & Vanities](/bathroom-countertops-vanities/).
- **Quartz** for rental and second homes where low maintenance matters most. See [Quartz Countertops](/quartz-countertops/).

### Working with builders and designers in Park City

We coordinate with general contractors, designers, and property managers on new construction and remodels, including slab holds, templating around cabinet installation, and scheduling that respects the mountain's weather and access limits. Ask about multi-room projects and remote-owner communication; we are used to sending photos and updates to owners who are not on site.

### How it works

Choose a slab at the Lindon shop or from photos we send you. We template on site, fabricate in Lindon, and install, usually in a single day for a kitchen, with old countertops removed and hauled away.
