## Bathroom Countertops, Vanity Tops, and Shower Surrounds

A bathroom is where stone gets to be beautiful first and practical second. Vanity tops see water, cosmetics, and toothpaste rather than hot pans and knives, which opens the door to materials and finishes that might be impractical in a kitchen. Graebridge Stone fabricates vanity tops, shower walls, tub decks, and niches in our Lindon shop and installs them across Utah County, the Salt Lake Valley, and the Park City and Heber area.

### What we build for bathrooms

- **Vanity tops** for single and double sinks, with undermount, vessel, or integrated sink options and polished backsplashes.
- **Full-height shower walls** in large-format porcelain or stone slabs, with far fewer grout lines than tile and nothing to scrub.
- **Tub decks and surrounds** with mitered edges and matching aprons.
- **Shower benches, curbs, and niches** cut from the same slab for a seamless look.
- **Furniture-style vanities** where the stone top sits on a freestanding piece.

### Choosing a material for a bath

[Marble](/quartzite-marble-countertops/) is the traditional choice and performs beautifully in a bath, where it rarely meets acids or heavy wear. [Quartz](/quartz-countertops/) gives the marble look with zero sealing, which many families prefer for kids' and guest baths. [Granite](/granite-countertops/) brings natural color and toughness to master baths. [Porcelain](/#porcelain) is our recommendation for shower walls: it is non-porous, UV-stable, and available in slabs large enough to clad a wall with a single seam.

### Vanity top or free-standing top?

A vanity top is cut to sit on a cabinet with a finished backsplash and side splashes as needed. A free-standing or furniture top finishes on all visible edges for a console or open-leg vanity. We fabricate both and will advise which suits your fixture and plumbing.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can you use the same stone on the vanity and in the shower?** Yes, and it is one of the most elegant things we do. Porcelain and quartz are the easiest to carry from countertop to wall.

**Is marble a bad idea in a bathroom?** No. Baths are where marble performs best. We seal it and show you how to maintain it.

**Do you do just the top, without replacing the vanity?** Yes. Retrofit vanity tops are common, and we template to your existing cabinet.
