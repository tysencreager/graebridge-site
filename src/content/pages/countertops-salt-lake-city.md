## Countertops for Salt Lake City and the Salt Lake Valley

Graebridge Stone templates and installs countertops throughout Salt Lake County from our fabrication shop in Lindon, about thirty minutes south of downtown. Salt Lake homeowners come to us for the same reasons our Utah County neighbors do: a family-owned shop that cuts its own stone, a certified porcelain installer when the project calls for it, and an installation crew that usually finishes a kitchen in one day.

### Cities we serve in Salt Lake County

Salt Lake City · Sandy · Draper · South Jordan · West Jordan · Riverton · Herriman · Bluffdale · Midvale · Murray · Holladay · Cottonwood Heights · Millcreek · Taylorsville · West Valley City · Kearns · Magna · South Salt Lake · Alta, and the surrounding communities. We also travel north to Davis County (Bountiful, Farmington, Kaysville, Layton) and west to Tooele County for the right project.

### Popular projects in the Salt Lake Valley

- **Quartz kitchens** in the newer South Jordan, Herriman, and Daybreak homes, often with waterfall islands. See [Quartz Countertops](/quartz-countertops/).
- **Granite and quartzite** in established Sandy, Holladay, and Cottonwood Heights kitchens, where natural stone suits the architecture. See [Granite](/granite-countertops/) and [Quartzite & Marble](/quartzite-marble-countertops/).
- **Marble and porcelain baths** in Salt Lake City's older neighborhoods and the Avenues, where a renovated bath is the whole project. See [Bathroom Countertops & Vanities](/bathroom-countertops-vanities/).
- **Full-height porcelain backsplashes and shower walls** for homeowners who want the look of a single seamless slab. See [Porcelain](/#porcelain).

### How a Salt Lake project works

You can visit the Lindon shop to choose a slab, or we can start from photos and measurements you text us. We template on site, fabricate in Lindon, and schedule installation around your remodel. Old countertops are removed and hauled away as part of the installation.
