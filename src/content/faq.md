Q: What types of countertops do you offer?
A: Granite, quartz, quartzite, marble, soapstone, and large-format porcelain. We carry slabs from Cambria, Caesarstone, Cosentino, Della Terra, MSI, Pental, and Stratus, and we can source most others on request.

Q: How do I choose the right countertop material for my kitchen or bathroom?
A: Start with how you live. Busy family kitchens usually lean quartz or quartzite; cooks who want natural variation choose granite; baths and bars are where marble shines. We walk you through samples in the Lindon shop and tell you plainly what each material will and will not tolerate.

Q: Do you offer custom countertops?
A: Yes. Every job is templated to your space and fabricated in our own shop, including waterfall islands, mitered edges, full-height backsplashes, fireplace surrounds, and furniture tops.

Q: How much do countertops cost?
A: Price depends on the material, the square footage, the edge profile, and the complexity of the installation. We provide free in-person estimates, and we will show you options at more than one price point.

Q: How long does countertop installation take?
A: Most installations are finished in a single day. Larger or more complex projects, such as multiple rooms or outdoor kitchens, can take longer, and we tell you the schedule before we start.

Q: What is the warranty on your countertops?
A: Manufacturer warranties vary by material and brand, and we stand behind our fabrication and installation work. Ask us about the specific warranty for the slab you choose.

Q: How should I care for my new countertop?
A: Mild soap and water handle daily cleaning for every material we sell. Natural stones like granite and marble benefit from periodic sealing; quartz and porcelain do not need it. We send care instructions with every installation.

Q: Can you remove my old countertops?
A: Yes. Removal and haul-away of your existing countertops is included with installation.

Q: Do you offer free estimates or consultations?
A: Yes. Consultations and estimates are complimentary, whether you visit the shop or send us photos and measurements.

Q: Do you offer installation services?
A: Yes. We template, fabricate, and install every countertop we sell with our own team, and we are certified to install large-format porcelain.
