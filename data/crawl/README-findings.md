# What the Screaming Frog crawl changed (Nov 19)

**Redirects.** The crawl found three URLs the sitemap did not list, all now in `redirects.csv` and `htaccess-redirects.txt` (130 rules total): `/hibu-eng-menu` (a noindexed Hibu popup page), `/project-consultation-and-design` (already a 404 but still linked internally), and `/first-last` (a 404 created by the blog byline placeholder, linked from every post). The 78 city pages are orphans: nothing on the site links to them, so Google only knows them from the sitemap. They keep their 301s because they are cheap and some may be indexed, but they carry no internal link equity.

**Where the old site's internal authority sits**, by inlink count: `/gallery` (810), home (485), `/contact` (387), `/serving-area` (324), `/request-a-call-back-form` (295), `/about` (207), `/kitchen-countertops` (205), `/reviews` (204). All of these map to home-page anchors or `/kitchen-countertops/`, so the new home page inherits almost all of it. `page-carryover.csv` has every old title, meta description, H1, word count, and inlink count beside its new target and new title, sorted by inlinks.

**Images.** Only 37 unique image files exist across the crawled pages: 4 logo variants, 4 Shutterstock stock photos (do not reuse), 2 Hibu "prq_" stock files, 1 placeholder, and about 26 client photos. The gallery page itself returned no images because it loads them with JavaScript, and the filenames show numbered series (`kitchen-gallery (1-12)`, `bathroom-gallery (11-28)`, `basement-renovation-gallery (3-8)`), so there are likely 40-70 more client photos on the CDN than the crawl saw. `download-images.ps1` (Windows) or `download-images.sh` (mac) pulls the 37 known files, tries the un-suffixed originals (Duda/Hibu usually keeps the full-size upload at the same path without `/opt/` and `-1920w`), and probes the numbered series up to 40 per set. 404s are silent. Run it from an empty folder; it leaves three subfolders: `found`, `originals`, `gallery-probe`. That replaces Nash's manual archive.

**Reviews.** The crawl ran without JavaScript rendering, so the reviews page came back with 221 words and no review text. Nash should pull reviews straight from the Google Business Profile rather than the old site.

**Hibu footer links.** Every page links to `budurl.com/hibuprivacy`, `hibuconditionsofuse`, `hibunotice`, `hibucookie` and `b.link/accessibility`. Those are Hibu's legal pages, not Graebridge's. The new site needs its own privacy policy (in the brief) and should drop the rest.

**Current title tags worth knowing.** Hibu pointed the home page at "Countertops Heber City, UT | Lehi, UT" and the kitchen page at "Countertops Park City, UT", while every other page says "Lindon, UT". The new titles put Lindon on the home page and give Park City and Heber their own area page, which is the right fix, but expect the Heber/Lehi impressions the home page currently gets to move around for a few weeks after launch.
